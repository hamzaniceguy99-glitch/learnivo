Why you freeze in meetings

It isn't vocabulary. You read the agenda fine. It's that producing language live is a separate skill, and you've had little safe practice.

How we work

Output, daily, with corrections

Adults who live in English all day often have strong passive skills and weak production. They follow conversations, then rehearse a sentence in their head until the moment passes.

The fix is output: daily short audio practice, a weekly voice note, and sessions where you speak far more than the coach. Corrections focus on what blocks meaning, so the conversation keeps moving.

And clarity, not accent removal. Being asked to repeat yourself usually traces to a few specific sounds or stress patterns. Fix those and the accent stops being a barrier.

Our principles

Three beliefs behind the method

Speak before you're ready

Waiting until the sentence is perfect is why the moment passes.

Ten minutes daily

Short, daily and out loud beats a long weekly study session.

Keep your accent

The goal is being understood, not sounding like someone else.

Twenty minutes of conversation, free, and an honest read on where to start.
