How we work

Your accent is not the problem

A lot of English teaching for adults quietly aims at sounding native. That's a bad goal: it's rarely achievable, it isn't necessary, and chasing it makes people speak less.

Learnivo aims at being understood the first time and saying what you mean. We fix the handful of sounds that cause repetition, build the vocabulary your job needs, and get you speaking far more than studying.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I've lived here for years and still avoid speaking.

Very common, and it's about practice under safe conditions rather than knowledge. Most people notice a difference within a few weeks of daily speaking.

Do you fix accents?

We work on clarity, not accent elimination. If people frequently ask you to repeat something, we find the specific sounds causing it — the rest of your accent stays.

Can you help with the citizenship interview?

We can practice conversation and listening in English. We are not immigration advisors and give no immigration or legal advice — that needs an accredited representative or attorney.

Do you prepare for TOEFL or IELTS?

We can work toward them, but we're not a test center and we issue no certification.

I only have 15 minutes a day.

That's enough for the audio practice, which is where most progress comes from. The weekly session does the rest.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
